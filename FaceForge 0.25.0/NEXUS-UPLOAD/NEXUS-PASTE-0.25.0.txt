FaceForge 0.25.0 - a photograph to an editable RaceMenu starting preset

FaceForge is an out-of-game Windows app. It measures a face in a photograph, locally, and writes a
RaceMenu preset pack you finish in game. It is not a Skyrim plugin, not an SKSE plugin, and it does not
belong in your load order, your Data folder, or your mod manager.

WHAT CHANGED IN 0.25.0

- Shape fitting is measured against the head you actually have. FaceForge renders your installed
  chargen head, measures how each slider moves it, then fits the photo's 468 landmarks to that measured
  response. The older ratio method is still there as "Legacy ratios".
- Colour is written, not just suggested. Skin tone (matched on Skyrim's own skin-tone gradient) and
  hair colour go into the preset; the swatches show what was read from the photo first.
- Vision accounts stop dead-ending. ChatGPT/Codex, Claude Code and Gemini CLIs are found even when the
  app is launched in a way that hides them, and Settings > Locate... lets you point at the program
  yourself. The path is remembered and shown.
- Morph data packed inside BSA archives is read, so High Poly Head and RaceMenu morph packages count.
- Mod Organizer 2 profiles and BCn-compressed skin textures are read directly.

INSTALL THE TOOL, NOT A GAME MOD

1. Unzip anywhere on the PC.
2. Run FaceForge-0.25.0-STANDALONE.exe.
3. Do not put it in Skyrim Special Edition\Data.
4. Do not install this archive with Vortex or Mod Organizer 2.

Windows 10/11 with WebView2. No .NET install needed, one single-file EXE.

Use it outside Mod Organizer 2 / USVFS. FaceForge detects that injection and says so rather than
failing on startup; run it normally and it still reads the same installed mods.

HOW TO USE IT

1. Choose a clear front photo. A slight turn is handled; a heavy turn or an open mouth is not reliable.
2. Analyze. Pick race and sex, then the installed head parts you want.
3. Optionally press "Refine with vision model" - that is the only step that sends anything off the PC,
   and it only happens if you configure an account and press the button.
4. Export the RaceMenu preset pack.
5. In Skyrim: open RaceMenu, load the preset, adjust if you like, and use Export Head if you plan to
   build a follower from it.
6. Optional: FollowerForge turns that exported head into an NPC plugin.

Compression of the face shape is conservative on purpose. Expect a starting point that is measurably
closer to the photo than the race default, not a finished likeness.

REQUIREMENTS

Hard: Windows 10/11, WebView2, Skyrim SE or AE, RaceMenu to load and export the preset.
Optional: High Poly Head, Expressive Facegen Morphs, and whatever head-part mods you already use.
Expressive Facegen Morphs matters: without it, RaceMenu silently ignores the EFM_ values and every
preset looks like the race default.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS, AS OF THIS UPLOAD

- No in-game likeness claim. The preset has not been loaded in RaceMenu by a person for this release;
  the numbers and the rendered preview are verified, the final look is not.
- Skin tone is matched by lightness only. Hue and makeup in the photo are not reproduced, and a
  strongly lit photo will read too light.
- Lip and iris colours are shown but not written. Eye parts are ranked by name, not by texture colour.
- One photo, single view. Depth is guessed; a turned or open-mouth photo leans on that guess.
- The sculpt output is four parametric moves, not a per-vertex fitted displacement.
- A face can come out uncanny. The fit has a conservative prior for that reason; adjust in RaceMenu.
