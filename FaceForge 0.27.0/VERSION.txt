FaceForge 0.27.0

Parent: FaceForge 0.26.0 (GitHub v0.26.0)
Status: GitHub release v0.27.0 (full release). No preset from it has been loaded in RaceMenu yet.
- Guided multi-view works: angle photos are judged by the head's measured turn in degrees (25-45° is
  ideal, 12-60° accepted). Before, only 2-15° passed, so every real three-quarter photo was ignored,
  and the side labels were inverted.
- Image quality is read from the face at the landmark model's own scale (resolution, blur by
  re-blurring, exposure, contrast). Before, a sharp photo, a blurred one and an upscaled thumbnail all
  scored 0.64-0.65 and good photos were called blurred.
- Match pane and vision refinement render each angle at the photo's measured turn and side.
- The automatic profile nose pass was removed: a round trip on the real head could not recover a known
  nose. Angled photos still give vertical proportions to the ratio fit and the analysis.
- Old unpacked interface copies (about 50 MB per build) are removed on start; a clear message when
  WebView2 is missing.
Verified: see VALIDATION.md (0.27.0 entry).
